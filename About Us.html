About Flash Delivery

100 Years of Speed, Trust & Innovation

Flash Delivery has been connecting people, businesses, and communities for over a century. What started as a small local delivery service has grown into a trusted name known for speed, reliability, and care.

Our Story

Founded in 1926, Flash Delivery began with one simple goal: deliver faster and smarter. Over the decades, we adapted to changing times, embraced technology, and expanded our reach — all while keeping our customers at the heart of everything we do.

Our Mission

To provide fast, affordable, and reliable delivery services while creating meaningful connections between people and businesses.

Our Vision

To be South Africa’s most trusted delivery service, setting the standard for speed, innovation, and customer satisfaction.
